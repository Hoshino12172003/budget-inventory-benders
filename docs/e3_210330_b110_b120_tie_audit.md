# E3 210330 B110/B120 tie audit

## Conclusion

`REPORTING_SCENARIO_SELECTION_TIE`

The B110 and B120 runs have the same certified economic solution to reporting precision. Their objective and exact robust recourse are identical. The saved worst-scenario shortage totals differ because two Gamma=2 scenarios attain the same worst recourse value and the reporting layer selected a different tied scenario.

This does not affect objective validity, feasibility, or the budget conclusion. The paper must not describe the saved shortage difference as service deterioration from B110 to B120.

## Exact fixed-state recourse recomputation

Two audit-only fixed-first-stage reporting evaluations were performed, one for each saved state. Both return exact robust recourse `590417.1700726735` and two tied worst scenarios.

1. Shocks to region 2 for `SLI---0770` and `SLI---1200`: total shortage is approximately `3104.4190`, shortage cost `222976.6028`, service penalty `365509.7672`, and transport cost `1930.8001`.
2. Shocks to region 2 for `SLI---1200` and region 5 for `CON-S-0130`: total shortage is approximately `3144.8047`, shortage cost `223696.6028`, service penalty `365179.2302`, and transport cost `1541.3371`.

The different component mixes sum to the same economic recourse. B110 stored the first scenario, while B120 stored the second. This is a reporting choice among tied maximizers, not a correctness inconsistency.
