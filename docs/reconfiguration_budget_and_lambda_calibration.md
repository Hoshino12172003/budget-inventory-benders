# Reconfiguration budget and lambda calibration

## Frozen reference budget

The new reference is the first-stage expenditure of the already frozen nominal
incumbent configuration:

\[
B_{ref}=\sum_i f_i y_i^0+\sum_{i,j}h_{ij}x^0_{ij}.
\]

This gives 84,614.30513135393 for 210202 and 50,558.18771213083 for
210628. No optimization is used to redefine these values. At \((y^0,x^0)\),
\(a^+=a^-=0\) and \(R(x^0;x^0)=0\), so the reference is independent of
\(\lambda_R\):

`BUDGET_REFERENCE_NONCIRCULAR = true`

The historical definition was the deterministic nominal unconstrained optimum's
first-stage spending. The new interpretation is the first-stage expenditure of
the frozen nominal incumbent configuration. Their numerical agreement is
expected because that configuration was generated by the same nominal model.
The reference does not depend on a robust optimum, a reconfiguration optimum, a
positive-\(\Gamma\) result, or any future formal experiment.

## Minimal calibration model

The implementation adds nonnegative \(a^+_{ij}\) and \(a^-_{ij}\), enforces

\[
x_{ij}-x^0_{ij}=a^+_{ij}-a^-_{ij},
\]

and prices their symmetric weighted magnitude as

\[
R(x;x^0)=\lambda_R\sum_{i,j}h_{ij}(a^+_{ij}+a^-_{ij}).
\]

The financial constraint applies to activation, final inventory, and
reconfiguration: \(C^{FS}\le B\). All depots retain their existing-facility
semantics, and \(x_{ij}\le UB_{ij}y_i\). No stock-conservation equation is added,
so additions, withdrawals, redistribution, and changes in total stock remain
possible.

Calibration uses a direct finite exact robust extensive form. It enumerates the
product-level binary demand shocks and all risk-budget allocations, then embeds
the original recourse LP for each finite case. This is a correctness/calibration
benchmark, not Product-wise Benders.

## Development design and predeclared selection

Both cases were run at \(\Gamma\in\{1,2\}\) and
\(\beta=B/B_{ref}\in\{0.90,1.00,1.10\}\). The initial suggested grid was
\(\{0,0.01,0.025,0.05,0.10,0.20,0.40,0.80,1.60\}\). A representative check
showed that the first positive value already reduced 210202 RI from 0.09732 to
0.02434 at the primary environment, leaving no useful low-friction point.
Therefore 0.0025 and 0.005 were added before the full grid was run.

The selection rule was fixed before the full grid: use \(\Gamma=2,\beta=1\),
normalize each case's RI by its \(\lambda_R=0\) RI, and target 0.60, 0.25, and
0.10 for LOW, MEDIUM, and HIGH. For each target, select a distinct increasing
positive grid value that keeps both normalized case responses in [0.02, 0.90]
and minimizes the maximum absolute deviation from the target across the two
cases. This yields the development recommendations:

- LOW: \(\lambda_R=0.0025\)
- MEDIUM: \(\lambda_R=0.05\)
- HIGH: \(\lambda_R=0.20\)

These are development recommendations, not frozen formal experiment levels.
The correctness and two-case response checks support freezing them in the next
formal-configuration step; this calibration PR does not itself perform that freeze.

## Results and mechanism checks

The full design contains 132 runs. Of these, 128 solved optimally. The four
\(\beta=0.90,\lambda_R=1.60\) combinations were infeasible across the two cases
and two \(\Gamma\) values. They are retained in the artifacts and are evidence
that the highest grid point is unsuitable under a tight budget.

Both cases have nonconstant RI responses. Small nonmonotonic movements occur at
some fixed environments and are retained; they coincide with discrete activation
or budget-driven inventory substitutions and are not treated as errors. The
selected levels remain reconfiguring and avoid the near-frozen/infeasible end of
the grid.

All 12 matched zero-friction comparisons (two cases, two \(\Gamma\) values,
three budgets) returned identical objective, activation, inventory, and robust
recourse within \(10^{-6}\):

`ZERO_FRICTION_EQUIVALENCE = true`

At \(\Gamma=0,\beta=1\), every positive grid value recovered the frozen
incumbent \(y^0,x^0\) within \(10^{-6}\):

`INCUMBENT_NOMINAL_RECOVERY = true`

The balance identity, budget and objective accounting, direct/adjustment RI
identity, and absence of materially simultaneous positive adjustments all pass.
The extensive form certifies robust recourse cost. Regional fill-rate statistics
are not reported because it does not select a unique global worst-case regional
service realization; no service statistic is fabricated.

This work changes neither the frozen \(x^0\) nor any Step 1--3 parameter. It does
not run a formal experiment and does not implement Product-wise Benders.
